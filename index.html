What we're building

Better conversations.
Clearer repair decisions.

A look at the planned OpenHood experience: practical tools to help your team explain the work and your customers feel informed.

01 / Inspect

Show the problem.

Capture and annotate 3D scans of worn parts. Let customers explore the damage visually and understand its severity without needing technical jargon.

02 / Update

Keep everyone in the loop.

Turn inspection photos, notes, and costs into a customer update in one step. Help service advisors see which vehicles are still waiting for approval.

03 / Decide

Make the next step clear.

Explain the problem, cause, urgency, impact, and recommended repair alongside photos and itemized costs. Customers can approve or decline each item remotely and explore the consequences of delay with an interactive urgency timeline.

04 / Explain

Talk it through.

Let customers ask their technician questions through chat or hear a live, plain-language audio explanation before deciding on a repair, even while they're away from the shop.

05 / Connect

Open a window into the bay.

Give customers a live view of diagnostics and repairs, with switchable camera angles. Bring messaging and one-tap work approvals into the same live experience.

06 / Welcome

Make the wait a little better.

Let waiting customers schedule lounge services, such as food delivery to their seat, through the app's timeline. Offer points for completed service that customers can redeem for a gift before they leave.
